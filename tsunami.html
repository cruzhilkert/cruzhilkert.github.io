HOW TO PLAY

You are a tsunami. Roll over the island and swallow everything smaller than you. Every thing you eat makes your wave bigger.

Move: WASD / arrow keys, or drag anywhere on the screen. On a phone use the joystick.

SURGE: press SPACE or the big button to dash — while surging your wave counts as 45% bigger, so you can gulp things you normally couldn't.

Too big? Anything you can't swallow yet has a 🔒 number floating over it — that's the size you need. You wash straight through it (nothing bounces you), so go eat smaller stuff and come back. The little things live out on the beach; the giants are downtown.

Rivals: other waves are eating too. Roll into a smaller wave to swallow it for a huge score. A bigger wave will eat you.

Getting eaten: you don't lose the run. You get put back at your spawn, a bit smaller, with a 🛡 one-minute spawn shield — nothing can touch you while the bubble is up, and the rivals won't even come after you. You can still eat everything on the island during it; you just can't swallow another wave until it runs out.

The rivals fight each other too. Whoever is bigger wins, same as with you. If two of them are scrapping on the far side of the island, that's your chance.

No clock. Nothing is timed. Play as long as you like and hit QUIT in the pause menu when you want to bank your score.

No powerups, no upgrades. Nothing to pick up off the ground and nothing to buy that makes you stronger. You and every rival wave run on exactly the same stats, every round.

Max size: every island has a size limit (it's on the island card) and it is a LOT higher than it used to be. Growing is slow on purpose — every bite gives you less the bigger you already are, and it gets steeper the bigger you get. Swallowing a rival is braked the same way, so you can't farm the little ones. The rival waves live under the identical rule, which is why nobody runs away with it. Hitting max size is meant to take a long, long run.

Game modes (pick one under the island):

🌊 CLASSIC — normal rules, no clock.
⚡ RUSH — double points and much faster growing.
⏳ MARATHON — 2 extra rivals and powerups everywhere.
👹 BOSS HUNT — a huge MEGA WAVE hunts you. Get big, then eat it for about 9,000 points.
💀 SURVIVAL — rivals start big and chase you. You have 3 ❤️; lose them all and the run ends.

Pets can't be bought. Each one is earned by finishing a mission — the pet tab tells you which. Once it's yours it swims next to your wave forever and gives a small bonus.

Missions pay out automatically the moment you finish them — check the results screen.

Everything you buy is saved on this device under your name, PLAYER.
